TOYZ autoart — brand hover sound sources

All recordings are from Freesound.org under Creative Commons 0 (public domain dedication,
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Attribution is not required; it is kept here for the record.
Clips were trimmed, high-passed, loudness-normalized (EBU R128) and faded by scripts in this project.

They are recordings of the same make or engine type, not of the specific cars in the TOYZ inventory.
Upload your own recording per brand in Admin > Brands.
(The longer per-car engine clips are no longer used on the site; they are kept in _source/engine-sounds.)
